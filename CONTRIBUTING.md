# Contributing to Awesome Startup Tools

This list is curated to a strict editorial standard. Every pull request is reviewed against the criteria below, and pull requests that do not meet them are closed.

## Inclusion criteria

A tool is accepted only when it meets all of the following:

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Credibility | Established, with a demonstrated track record of reliable operation, real-world usage, and continued availability |
| Domain | Hosted on its own custom domain. Links to GitHub repositories or platform subdomains (for example netlify.app or vercel.app) are not accepted |
| Pricing | Offers a permanent free plan or a permanently usable free tier. Free trials do not qualify |
| Relevance | Useful to startups, indie hackers, entrepreneurs, or small teams |

New or recently launched tools are generally not accepted until they meet the credibility criterion.

## How to submit

1. You may add one or more tools in a pull request. Each tool must meet the inclusion criteria on its own.
2. Place each tool in the most relevant existing category.
3. Use the existing format: tool name, a hyphen, then the URL.
4. Do not add tracking parameters or referral links.
5. Only add new tools. Do not combine additions with edits, removals, new categories, or other changes.

## Review process

Every pull request receives an automated review against the criteria above. Submissions that fail it are rejected. Passing it is not an approval: approval requires a final review by the maintainer, who has sole discretion to accept or decline any submission, in full or in part.

## Quality cleanup

Existing entries are reviewed periodically. Entries that no longer meet the criteria above are removed.

## Featured listings

Pull requests are reviewed only against the inclusion criteria above. Payment does not change the outcome of a review, and inclusion in the curated list cannot be purchased.

Any eligible product, including newer products that do not yet meet the curated criteria, may apply for a featured listing, labelled as Sponsored. See [SPONSORS.md](SPONSORS.md).
