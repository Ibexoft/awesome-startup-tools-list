# Featured Listings on Awesome Startup Tools

Place your product in front of founders, indie hackers, and small teams at the point where they are choosing their tool stack.

Awesome Startup Tools is a curated GitHub list of apps, SaaS products, and online services for startups. Featured listings are open to any eligible product. No pull request is required.

## What a featured listing includes

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Placement | One line in the Featured section at the top of the README |
| Content | Product name, link, and a description of up to 15 words |
| Link | Any URL you choose, including your own tracking parameters |
| Labelling | Marked as Sponsored, in line with GitHub's advertising policy and the FTC Endorsement Guides |
| Availability | Limited to 5 featured listings at any time |
| Terms | Monthly, quarterly, or annual, paid in advance |

## Eligibility

A featured listing is available to any product that:

- Is live and usable, with a working sign-up or download
- Is hosted on the product's own custom domain
- Is relevant to startups, founders, developers, or small teams

The following are not accepted: adult content, gambling, weapons, investment or get-rich-quick schemes, malware, impersonation, or any product that breaches GitHub's Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policies. Acceptance is at the maintainer's sole discretion.

## How to request a quote

1. Email **support@ibexoft.com** with the subject line **Featured listing**. Include your product URL, your company name, your preferred term, and your proposed one-line description.
2. Your product is reviewed and, if approved, you receive a quote.
3. On acceptance, you receive a Payoneer payment request by email. Payment must be made from a business account, by business card or business bank transfer. No Payoneer account is required.
4. Your listing is published once payment is received.

For monthly terms, a new payment request is sent before each renewal. The listing continues while each request is paid.

## Editorial independence

Featured listings are separate from the curated list. Payment does not influence whether a tool is added to, kept in, or removed from the curated categories, and inclusion in the curated list cannot be purchased.

## Terms

- All fees are paid in advance and are non-refundable.
- Listings run for the paid term and are removed at the end of the term unless renewed.
- The maintainer may remove a listing at any time if the product no longer meets the eligibility criteria above or GitHub's policies.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to submit a pull request first?**
No. Any eligible product can request a quote directly by email.

**Can I pay to be added to the curated list?**
No. The curated list is editorial. Paid placement is available only in the labelled Featured section.

**Is my product eligible if it is newly launched?**
Yes, provided it meets the eligibility criteria above. The curated list's track-record requirement does not apply to featured listings.

**Can I change my description during the term?**
Yes. Email support@ibexoft.com with the new text.
